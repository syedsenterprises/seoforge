What the free audit checks

  • Title tag: whether your page has one, and whether it is a sensible length. Missing or empty titles are one of the most common basic errors.
  • Meta description: whether it exists and reads like a real summary of the page. Google often rewrites it, but a good one still helps clicks.
  • Headings: whether your page has a single clear H1 and a sensible heading structure underneath it.
  • Images: whether your images carry alt text, so search engines and screen readers know what they show.
  • Speed score: SEOForge gives your page a 0–100 speed score from its own measurement. It is an honest estimate, not a lab-grade Lighthouse number — the page says so.
  • Basic technical checks: things like whether the page is reachable, whether it has a canonical URL, and whether the basics a crawler needs are in place.

What it does not do

SEOForge shows you only what it actually measured. It does not invent a backlink count, a domain authority score, or keyword volumes it has no data for. If a tool shows you those numbers for free, ask where they came from — most are estimates, and some are made up. Our audit sticks to what can be checked on the live page.

How to use your audit result

  1. Fix the red items first. Missing titles, missing meta descriptions and missing H1s are quick wins.
  2. Fix the images. Add alt text that describes the image in a few words.
  3. Re-run the audit. After you change something, run the audit again to confirm the fix landed.
  4. Save it. Create a free account to keep your audits, track the same pages over time, and connect Search Console to see your real clicks and impressions next to the audit.

How often should you audit?

Once after every big change to a page, and once a month for your most important pages. An audit is a snapshot, not a one-time cure — pages drift as you edit them, and Google's view of them changes too.
