Free Rank Tracker — Track Your Google Rankings

SEOForge by Syeds Enterprises · Free plan

Rank tracking is watching where your pages sit in Google for the keywords you care about. SEOForge tracks your rankings over time, so you can see whether your work is moving the needle — or whether a page is slipping and needs attention.

Set it up: create a free account, open the SEOForge app, add the keywords that matter to your site, and check back weekly.

Which keywords to track

  • Your money pages first. The pages that bring enquiries or sales — track the keyword each one is built for.
  • Five to twenty keywords is enough. A small site does not need to track hundreds. Track the ones tied to real pages.
  • Include one or two you do not rank for yet. Watching a new page climb from nowhere is the clearest proof your SEO is working.

How to read the numbers

  • Positions move daily. A jump from 12 to 9 in a day is normal noise, not a victory. Look at the trend over two to four weeks.
  • Page 2 to page 1 is the big move. Going from position 11 to 8 roughly doubles the clicks a page gets. That is where your effort pays best.
  • A falling position is a to-do list. If a page slides for three weeks, re-read it: is the content stale, is the title weak, has a competitor published something better?
  • Rankings differ by location and device. A position is a snapshot from one place on one device. Treat it as a guide, not a precise measurement.

Rank tracking plus Search Console

Connect Google Search Console in the app and your tracked keywords sit next to your real clicks and impressions. That combination — the position you track, and the clicks Google actually sent you — is the honest picture. A keyword at position 6 with almost no clicks usually means the title is not earning the click, not that the ranking is wrong.
