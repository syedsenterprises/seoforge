How to pick keywords that work

  1. Start with what you actually do. Write down the words a customer would type to find you. A bakery in Hyderabad would start with "bakery in Hyderabad", not "best cakes in the world".
  2. Prefer specific over broad. "Free SEO audit tool" is a better target for a small site than "SEO", which is fought over by companies with huge budgets. Specific phrases — long-tail keywords — bring fewer visitors, but visitors who actually want what you offer.
  3. Match the page to the word. One page, one main keyword. If two pages chase the same word, they compete with each other.
  4. Use the word naturally. Put it in the title, the first paragraph, and a heading or two. Do not stuff it into every sentence — Google reads that as spam, and readers bounce.
