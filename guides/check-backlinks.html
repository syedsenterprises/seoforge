Links are still one of the strongest signals Google uses. But a link only helps if it is actually live, on a real page, and pointing at the right URL. SEOForge's Link Verifier checks all of that — for any link you give it — and tells you plainly what it found.

Check a link now: open the SEOForge app, go to the Link Verifier, and paste the URL of the page the link sits on.

What the Link Verifier checks

  • Is the link live? Whether the linking page loads at all, or returns an error.
  • Does it point where you think? Whether the link's destination is the URL you expect, or whether it redirects somewhere else.
  • Is it follow or nofollow? A nofollow link still sends visitors, but it does not pass ranking value the way a follow link does. Knowing which one you got matters before you pay for it.

Before you pay for a link, verify it

Paid link listings are a normal part of SEO, and SEOForge itself runs a link marketplace where sites can buy a listing for $20. Whatever the price, the rule is the same: check the link after it goes live. Sellers sometimes place a link and remove it a week later, or put it on a page Google never indexes. A two-minute check protects the money. That is exactly what the Verifier is for — it is the same check we recommend for our own marketplace listings.

What SEOForge does not claim

SEOForge does not have a backlink index — a database of every link on the web — and it will not show you a backlink count or a domain authority score for your site. Those databases cost a fortune to build and maintain, and any free tool showing you exact backlink numbers is estimating. What the Verifier does is narrower and honest: it checks the specific links you ask about, live, and reports what it actually found.
