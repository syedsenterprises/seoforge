Privacy Policy

SEOForge by Syeds Enterprises · Effective 23 November 2026 · Contact: contact@syedsenterprises.org

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase). We never see your password.
  • Content you enter: audits, keywords, rank checks, revenue entries, notes and preferences you save in SEOForge.
  • Google data (only if you connect Google): read-only access to your Search Console and Google Analytics data, using the scopes you approve. We only read this data when you ask for it inside the app, and you can disconnect Google at any time.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by PayPal. We receive your subscription status (for example, whether your Pro plan is active). We never receive or store your card numbers.
  • Emails: we send account emails (such as sign-in confirmation and password recovery) and, only if you opt in, a weekly summary of your saved data.

How we use it

We use your data only to run SEOForge: to sign you in, to show your saved work back to you, to manage your subscription, and to send the emails you asked for. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Who else sees your data

Your data passes through the services that run SEOForge — Supabase (authentication and database), Google (only the Search Console and Analytics data you connect), PayPal (payments) and Resend (emails) — and only as needed to provide the service. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

If you use the AI assistant, your messages and your own API key are sent to the AI provider you chose in Settings (CoArena, Google Gemini, or your own OpenAI-compatible provider). SEOForge never stores your key or your chats.

Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored in our Supabase project (Mumbai, India region). Your browser also stores a session and your display preferences locally on your own device.

Your rights

You can disconnect Google or PayPal from inside the app at any time. You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account and its data to be deleted, by emailing contact@syedsenterprises.org. We will act on deletion requests promptly, except for the billing records we must keep for accounting.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@syedsenterprises.org.
