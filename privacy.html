Privacy Policy

SEOForge by Syeds Enterprises · Effective 25 November 2026 · Contact: contact@syedsenterprises.org

SEOForge is run by Syed Enterprises, based in Hyderabad, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 with the IT Rules, 2021, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA). This is a plain-language policy, not legal advice, and it does not claim any legal certification.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase). We never see your password.
  • Content you enter: audits, keywords, rank checks, revenue entries, notes, link listings and preferences you save in SEOForge.
  • URLs you audit: when you run a free or saved audit, the URL you submit is fetched by our audit service so we can report on it. The URL and its results are only stored if you are signed in and save the audit; a free audit run without an account is not tied to you.
  • Google data (only if you connect Google): read-only access to your Search Console and Google Analytics data, using the scopes you approve. We only read this data when you ask for it inside the app, and you can disconnect Google at any time.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by PayPal. We receive your subscription status (for example, whether your Pro plan is active). We never receive or store your card numbers.
  • Emails: we send account emails (such as sign-in confirmation and password recovery) and, only if you opt in, a weekly summary of your saved data.
  • On your own device: your browser stores a sign-in session and your display preferences (theme, accent) locally. If you use SEOForge without an account, your work is stored only in your own browser and never leaves it.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or data from other websites you visit. We run no analytics and set no analytics cookies on the site today; if that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first (see Cookies below).

How we use it — and the lawful basis (GDPR)

We use your data only to run SEOForge: to sign you in, to show your saved work back to you, to manage your subscription, and to send the emails you asked for. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

  • Contract — providing the account and the tools you signed up for.
  • Consent — connecting Google, and receiving optional emails such as the weekly summary. You can withdraw consent at any time (disconnect Google, or unsubscribe).
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure and working, and preventing abuse. We do not use your data for advertising.

Cookies

SEOForge uses only cookies and local storage that are strictly necessary for the service to work: the sign-in session (set by Supabase) and your saved preferences (theme, accent). We set no advertising or analytics cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will ask for your consent through a cookie banner first, and you will be able to decline without losing access to the free tools. You can clear stored data at any time from your browser settings; signing out ends the session. Our Cookie notice is this section — there is no separate cookie policy.

Who else sees your data

Your data passes through the services that run SEOForge — Supabase (authentication and database), Google (only the Search Console and Analytics data you connect), PayPal (payments) and Resend (emails) — and only as needed to provide the service.

If you use the AI assistant, your messages and your own API key are sent to the AI provider you chose in Settings (CoArena, Google Gemini, or your own OpenAI-compatible provider). SEOForge never stores your key or your chats. Questions about your own saved data that the assistant can answer without an AI call are answered in your browser and sent nowhere.

Where your data is stored and transferred

Your account data is stored in our Supabase project (Mumbai, India region). The service providers above may process data outside India and, for visitors from the EU/UK, outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, it is under those providers' own terms and safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses). Under the DPDP Act, we do not transfer personal data to any country the Government of India has notified as restricted.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data only while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, your saved data is deleted, except for the billing records we must keep for accounting and tax purposes. Data stored only in your browser is deleted when you clear it.

Your rights

You can exercise the rights below by emailing contact@syedsenterprises.org. We will respond within 30 days. There is no fee for a reasonable request.

  • India (DPDP Act, 2023): as a Data Principal you may ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or erase it; you may withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing consent may mean some features stop working); you may nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to; and you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (below) or complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • EU/UK (GDPR): you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing, including objecting to any processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Even where the CCPA/CPRA may not strictly apply to a business of our size, we honour these requests.

You can also disconnect Google or PayPal from inside the app at any time, and you can delete your account from the app or by email.

Children's data

SEOForge is not aimed at children. Under the DPDP Act we do not knowingly process personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent, and we do not track children, show them targeted advertising, or monitor their behaviour. If you are a parent and believe your child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Grievance redressal (India)

Under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, our Grievance Officer is: Syed Shahed, Syed Enterprises — contact@syedsenterprises.org. We acknowledge grievances within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act, 2023.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the effective date above. If a change is material, we will also note it in the app.
