Data sources & honest limits

  • Site Audit: your sitemap fetched through a free public proxy; PageSpeed Insights (Google, free, no key). Google's no-key quota is shared and often exhausted — if it returns 429, add a free API key in Ad Revenue → Settings, or retry later.
  • Keyword Explorer: live suggestions from Google Suggest. Search volume is not available without a paid keyword API — it is never invented here.
  • Rank Tracker: checks DuckDuckGo results, not Google. Position is honest for that source only.
  • Link Verifier: reads the live linking page and reports the real rel attribute (dofollow / nofollow / sponsored / ugc).
  • Ad Revenue: manual logging only in v2. Network synchronization is not currently available.
  • Backlink index: there is no free Ahrefs/Semrush backlink index. SEOForge does not fake one — verify links directly with the Link Verifier.
  • Keyword Difficulty: an estimate from the live DuckDuckGo top 10 (authority domains, exact-match titles, .gov/.edu) — not a paid index.
  • Redirect chains: free proxies follow redirects on their own servers, so intermediate hops are only shown when the proxy exposes them; the final URL is shown when it can be read.
  • Revenue Estimator: typical published AdSense RPM ranges by niche — an estimate, never a promise.
  • Content briefs: headings and word counts come from reading the live top 5 pages; questions come from Google Suggest.

SERP Preview + Meta / OG Checker

See how a page looks in Google results and when shared on social media. Reads the page's real title, meta description and Open Graph / Twitter tags.

Broken Link Checker

Fetches a page, lists every link on it and checks each one live. Up to 100 links per check. A “proxy error” means the free proxy could not reach that link — it does not prove the link is broken.

Redirect Chain Checker

Follows a URL's redirects and shows every hop. Honest note: free proxies follow redirects on their own servers and do not always expose the intermediate hops — when that happens this shows the final URL and status, and says so.

Schema / JSON-LD Detector

Finds every structured-data block on a page and lists its type — Article, FAQPage, Product, LocalBusiness and more — with the raw JSON-LD to inspect.

Robots.txt Tester

Fetches a site's robots.txt, shows it, and tests whether a path is allowed. Rules are matched the way search engines do: longest matching rule wins, Allow beats Disallow on a tie.

Robots.txt Generator

Build a basic robots.txt to copy or download. No fetching needed — this does not count against your tool-check limit.

Sitemap Generator

Crawls a site's internal links (up to 50) from its homepage and builds a sitemap.xml you can download. Only links found on the homepage are included — pages linked from deeper pages are not.

Content Brief Generator

Enter a keyword. SEOForge reads the top 5 ranking pages (their headings and length), pulls real question suggestions, and builds a brief: headings to cover, questions to answer, pages to beat, and a target word count (median of the top 5, rounded up — labelled as an estimate).

Competitor Compare

Two URLs side by side: PageSpeed score, title and meta description, H1 count and word count. PageSpeed uses Google's free quota — if it returns 429, the exact error is shown and the on-page numbers are still compared.

Competitors you track

Ad Revenue Estimator

Estimate what your traffic could earn from display ads. This is an estimate from typical published AdSense RPM ranges — not a promise. Actual earnings depend on traffic country, ad placement, season and network.

SEO Health Checklist

Seven live checks on one URL: HTTPS, title length, meta description, exactly one H1, image alt text, viewport tag, and mobile PageSpeed score. The PageSpeed check counts as one of your monthly site audits.

Bulk Audit

Run PageSpeed on up to 5 URLs in one go. Each URL counts as one site audit against your monthly limit. If Google's free PageSpeed quota is exhausted, the exact error is shown per URL — no fake scores.
