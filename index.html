Why SEOForge — and why it costs $9, not $139

Semrush charges $139/month. We charge $9. That is not a discount — it is a different market, and it is huge.

Price — $9 vs $139

We are for the people Semrush prices out: freelancers, students, small publishers, bloggers in India and other price-sensitive markets. That is our market, and it is huge.

Tools + marketplace in one place

Semrush has no link marketplace. We do — buy and sell link placements ($20 listings) and verify with the Link Verifier that a paid link is really live on the page with its real rel attribute. Tools bring users in; the marketplace is what makes us different.

No friction

Free tier with no card, and most tools work with no account at all. Semrush makes you sign up and pay before you see anything useful.

Honest numbers

Every score is measured or labelled as an estimate. No invented search volumes, no faked backlink index. In a market full of inflated claims, that is the trust play.

Light and fast

The whole app is ~200KB — it loads instantly on a cheap phone on 4G. Semrush's app is heavy.

What we don't have (yet) — and we won't hide it: we do NOT have a backlink index or a keyword-volume database. That is what the $139 buys at Semrush, and our $9 reflects it. We never claim "Semrush power" or "Ahrefs alternative" as if the data were equal. The honest pitch is: everything a small site needs to audit, track, and earn — for $9.

Why trust this — don't take our word for it

Don't trust our word — check it. Verification is built into the product:

Measured by Google's own API

PageSpeed scores come from Google PageSpeed Insights — Google's API, not our own numbers. Our Speed Score is our own measurement, and it says so on the report.

Every number shows its raw inputs

Each Speed Score sub-score shows its real measurement — response time in milliseconds, page weight in kilobytes, the actual counts. No score without its evidence.

Verify it yourself in one click

Every PageSpeed score links to Google's own PageSpeed tool, so you can compare — same API, so it matches. Every verified link is clickable to the live page.

Free audit first, no card

Run the audit above on your own site before paying anything. If the numbers don't match what you know about your site, don't pay — the check is free either way.

We admit what we can't do

No backlink index, no search volumes, no true LCP/CLS without a real browser render — the limits are stated in the UI, not hidden. Admitting what we can't do is what makes the rest believable.

Questions, answered plainly

Is it really free?

Yes — the free plan needs no card and no trial. It has monthly limits (5 audits, 3 briefs, 3 compares, 10 tool checks, 10 keywords, 5 AI messages a day, 1 site). Pro is $9/month for unlimited use.

Where does the data come from?

Real public sources only: your own sitemap, Google PageSpeed Insights, Google Suggest, Bing search results (DuckDuckGo as a fallback), and the live pages themselves. Search volume and backlink counts are not shown, because no free source provides them — they are never invented.

Do I need an account?

No. Continue without an account and every tool works in your browser (localStorage). Sign in free to save your audits, keywords and revenue to your account and use them on any device.

What happens to my data?

Signed-in data lives in your own Supabase account, visible only to you. Logged-out data never leaves your browser. Google tokens are stored server-side, never in the page. Export everything anytime from Workspace settings.

What about the AI assistant?

The in-site assistant ("Ask Forge") runs on your own AI key — CoArena, or a free Gemini key. SEOForge stores no key server-side and pays nothing for your chats. Without a key it still answers questions about your own saved data, free.

Why should I trust the scores?

Don't take our word for it — check it. PageSpeed scores come from Google's own API, and every score links to Google's PageSpeed tool so you can compare. Every number shows its raw inputs, every verified link is clickable to the live page, and the limits are stated in the UI. Run the free audit above on your own site first.
